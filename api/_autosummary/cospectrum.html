cospectrum

computes (co)spectrum of two variables, from InnFlux code for spectrum, simply input the same time series for x and y
cospectrum.cospectrum(ini, x, y, f, mean_wind_speed, plot=False, filename='', spectrum_type='')[source]

computes (co)spectrum of two variables, from InnFlux code for spectrum, simply input the same time series for x and y

Parameters:

  • ini (dict, initialisation information)

  • x (numpy arr of float, fluctuations of the first time series)

  • y (numpy arr of float, fluctuations of the second time series)

  • f (numpy arr of float, frequency axis of cospectra [s-1])

  • mean_wind_speed (float, mean wind speed [m s-1])

  • spectrum_type (str, text to be used in the plot (``’spec’`` or 'cospec'))

Returns:

  • cospec_xy (numpy arr of float, (co)spectrum for xy, Co(x``’,y’``,f))

  • cospec_xy_scaled (numpy arr of float, scaled (co)spectrum f*Co(x``’,y’``,f)/xy)

  • cospec_xy_integral (numpy arr of float, integral of (co)spectrum)
